## A mathematical perspective

For a closed, oriented Riemannian surface $M$, the Gauss–Bonnet theorem relates curvature to topology:

$$
\int_M K\,dA = 2\pi\chi(M).
$$

Here $K$ is the Gaussian curvature and $\chi(M)$ is the Euler characteristic.
